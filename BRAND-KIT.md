# Thanh Le Church Safety Policy Brand Kit

## Brand idea

**Clear standards. Shared responsibility. Faithful care.**

The policy website should feel warm, pastoral, trustworthy, and accountable. Its visual system takes color cues from the Thanh Le Church stained-glass mark while keeping the reading experience calm and procedural.

## Color palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Primary use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Heritage Red | `#A51A15` | Links, active navigation, branded accents |
| Dark primary | Deep Red | `#6B120E` | Tabs, footer, dark branded surfaces |
| Accent | Ember Orange | `#D95B1C` | Focus rings and gradient details |
| Decorative | Stained-Glass Gold | `#EA8F2D` | Restrained highlights only |
| Background | Warm Canvas | `#FFF9F4` | Site background |
| Surface | Paper White | `#FFFFFF` | Policy reading surface |
| Text | Ink | `#241A17` | Body copy and headings |
| Secondary text | Muted Ink | `#645752` | Metadata and supporting copy |
| Border | Soft Border | `#E8D9D0` | Rules, cards, tables, and separators |

### Functional colors

| Meaning | Hex |
|---|---|
| Information | `#2F5E73` |
| Recommended practice / adopted status | `#287A52` |
| Warning / unresolved decision | `#8A4B00` |
| Immediate safety concern | `#B42318` |

Functional colors communicate meaning and should not be replaced with Heritage Red merely to match the church brand.

## Typography

- Policy headings: Source Serif 4, 600–700 weight
- Body and interface: DM Sans
- Code and technical references: Roboto Mono

## Logo use

- Use the full-color church mark on white, warm white, or near-black surfaces.
- Do not recolor, stretch, crop, add effects, or place it directly on red or orange.
- Keep the master artwork separate from the optimized web asset.
- The website header uses a white badge and subtle border to protect legibility.

## Interface principles

- Use red and orange as accents, not as large reading surfaces.
- Keep the policy body on a white, paper-like surface.
- Use functional callout colors according to meaning.
- Favor modest `6–8px` corner radii and minimal shadows.
- Preserve visible keyboard focus and sufficient text contrast.
- Avoid stock photographs of children or youth.

## Policy status labels

| Status | Color family |
|---|---|
| Draft for leadership review | Amber |
| Adopted policy | Green |
| Superseded version | Gray |
| Urgent safety information | Red |

## Website entry points

The homepage should prioritize three tasks:

1. Read the policy.
2. Open the Immediate Response Guide.
3. Find the Reporting and Escalation Directory.
