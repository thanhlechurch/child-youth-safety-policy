# Section 14: Attendance, Check-In, Check-Out, and Release

## Section 14.1: Children’s Ministry

TLC shall use its approved electronic attendance and secure-release system, currently KidCheck, or an approved replacement. A parent, legal guardian, or authorized adult must complete or affirm check-in. A child may not independently check in by entering the family phone number.

- An approved staff member or volunteer must knowingly receive the child before the responsible adult departs.

- The child shall wear or retain the issued identification as required by the ministry procedure.

- Relevant allergy, medical, custody, and photo restrictions must be communicated discreetly to staff and volunteers with a need to know.

- At release, an approved staff member or volunteer verifies the matching printed or electronic security credential before the child leaves.

- Familiarity with the adult is not a substitute for verification.

- If the credential is missing, the child remains supervised while a designated coordinator verifies identification and authorized-pickup status and documents the exception.

## Section 14.2: Centralized CM checkout

After classroom lessons, CM classes return together to the CM Main Room for centralized checkout. Teachers count children before leaving the classroom and again upon arrival. Children remain actively supervised until released. No child may wait alone in a hallway, stairway, restroom, or classroom.

## Section 14.3: Youth attendance and release

Youth Ministry shall maintain accurate attendance and emergency contact information for regular and special activities. Parent authorization is required for off-site, transportation, overnight, and other designated events. The ministry handbook shall define when youth may self-arrive or self-dismiss based on age, parent authorization, location, and risk.
