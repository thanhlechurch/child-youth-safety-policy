# Section 13: Visibility, Facilities, and Monitoring

- Classroom windows, vision panels, and other visibility features must remain unobstructed while minors are present. Curtains or blinds remain open except during an emergency lockdown or other documented safety need.

- Doors remain unlocked and, when visibility is limited, open. Private areas, closets, backstage spaces, storage rooms, mechanical rooms, and unused rooms are off-limits unless specifically authorized and supervised.

- Hallways, stairways, entrances, exits, restrooms, playgrounds, and transition zones must be actively monitored.

- Ministry leaders shall conduct room counts and final sweeps after programs and events.

- Cameras may be used in public or ministry spaces when approved, disclosed, and secured. Cameras and personal devices are prohibited in restrooms, showers, changing areas, or other places where privacy is expected.

- The Safeguarding Leads or other Board-designated persons may conduct announced and unannounced observations. Staff and volunteers may not obstruct or retaliate against monitoring.

Facilities and equipment must be inspected regularly. Emergency exits, fire extinguishers, first-aid kits, AEDs, and evacuation routes must be identifiable and accessible.
