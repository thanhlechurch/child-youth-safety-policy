# Section 15: Restrooms, Diapering, and Personal Care

- Staff and volunteers shall supervise travel to and from restrooms. Children may not wander unsupervised through hallways or isolated areas.

- A screened adult checks the restroom or route when appropriate and remains outside or at the doorway while preserving privacy.

- Children use individual stalls; staff and volunteers should prevent children from entering stalls together unless an individual support need requires a documented exception.

- A staff member or volunteer may not be alone inside a restroom with one child when assistance is required. Another approved adult must be present or able to observe the assistance while privacy is preserved.

- Staff and volunteers provide only the minimum necessary assistance and explain what they are doing in age-appropriate language.

- Diapering occurs only at a designated station, in view of another approved adult, with sanitation procedures and documentation as required by the CM handbook.

- Parents should be contacted for wiping, changing, or care beyond the approved procedure unless a written individual support plan authorizes trained assistance.

- Staff and volunteers may not use the restroom, shower, or change clothes in the presence of minors under their supervision.
