# Section 26: Bullying, Hazing, Harassment, and Peer-to-Peer Misconduct

TLC prohibits bullying, harassment, hazing, sexualized conduct, coercion, exploitation, image sharing, threats, stalking, fighting, and abuse by one minor against another.

- Staff and volunteers must intervene promptly and safely, separate participants, protect potential evidence, and notify the responsible leader.

- Do not require an alleged victim to confront, forgive, apologize to, or reconcile with another participant as a condition of safety or participation.

- Sexual behavior involving coercion, force, significant age or developmental differences, exploitation, penetration, injury, explicit images, or other reasonable suspicion of abuse must be reported to civil authorities as required.

- Both the harmed child and the child who caused harm may need professional assessment, safety planning, and pastoral support. Safety plans must not minimize accountability.
