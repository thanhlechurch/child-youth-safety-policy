# Section 25: Camps, Retreats, Overnight Events, and Mission Trips

- Complete an event-specific risk assessment, approval, staffing plan, medical and emergency plan, transportation plan, communication plan, and parent consent process.

- All adult leaders must complete required screening and training before departure.

- Use at least two approved adults and adequate ratios for each activity, vehicle, lodging area, and free-time period.

- Separate sleeping, changing, and shower arrangements by age, privacy need, and event plan. Adults do not share beds or sleeping bags with minors.

- No adult may be alone in a hotel room, cabin, tent, bedroom, or other sleeping area with one minor.

- Room or bed checks are conducted by two approved adults without unnecessary touching.

- Maintain rules for curfew, free time, swimming, medication, illness, emergency contact, prohibited items, technology, and leaving the site.

- International mission trips require review of destination law, medical care, passports, insurance, communications, emergency evacuation, and local partner screening.
