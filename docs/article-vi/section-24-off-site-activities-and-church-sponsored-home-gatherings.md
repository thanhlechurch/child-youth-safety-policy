# Section 24: Off-Site Activities and Church-Sponsored Home Gatherings

## Section 24.1: Event approval

Off-site activities require advance approval, a responsible leader, adequate staffing, parent authorization when applicable, attendance and emergency information, transportation planning, site assessment, and a communication plan.

## Section 24.2: Home gatherings

- Use screened host adults and approved ministry leaders.

- Provide parents with the address, leaders, start and end times, transportation plan, and contact information.

- Use common areas; bedrooms, closed offices, garages, and isolated areas are off-limits.

- Maintain attendance, supervision, restroom, communication, and release standards equivalent to church property.

- No alcohol, cannabis, illegal drugs, or impairing substance may be used by supervising adults during the gathering.
