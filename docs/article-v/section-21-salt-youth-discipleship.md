# Section 21: SALT Youth Discipleship

SALT is TLC’s approved discipleship program. As a program-wide rule, all SALT pairings are same-gender, including adult-to-adult and adult-to-youth pairings. Within Youth, an adult male discipler may be paired only with a male youth disciple, and an adult female discipler may be paired only with a female youth disciple. Adults may not be assigned through SALT to individually disciple children in nursery or CM.

## Section 21.1: Approval and consent

- Every adult discipler of a minor must complete the screening, background check, training, Youth handbook, SALT addendum, and acknowledgment requirements assigned to the role.

- The parent or guardian must receive written information about the match, meeting expectations, communication methods, transportation limits, reporting contacts, and the right to request reassignment or end the relationship.

- Written parent or guardian consent is required before recurring individual discipleship begins.

- SALT leadership maintains a current list of matches and conducts regular contact with the discipler, youth, and parent or guardian.

## Section 21.2: Meeting structure

Small-group discipleship is preferred. Approved one-to-one mentoring may occur only under the California one-to-one mentoring exception and TLC safeguards, including comprehensive screening, training for the mentor and parent, public or observable meetings, regular oversight, and prompt external reporting procedures.

- Meet during reasonable hours in public or observable common areas.

- Another approved adult or the parent knows the location and expected duration.

- No meetings in bedrooms, hotel rooms, isolated portions of homes, or parked vehicles.

- Home meetings require another responsible adult present and use of common areas only.

- Individual rides are not automatically authorized by the SALT match and must comply with the transportation policy.

## Section 21.3: Spiritual and emotional boundaries

- Do not cultivate exclusivity, dependency, loyalty, secrecy, or the belief that the discipler understands the youth better than parents or other trusted adults.

- Do not undermine parents, isolate the youth from peers or leaders, give excessive or secret gifts, or use the youth as the adult’s emotional confidant.

- Do not disclose adult marital, sexual, financial, or intimate struggles to the youth.

- Do not use spiritual authority to pressure disclosure, obedience, affection, forgiveness, reconciliation, or continued contact.

- A youth or parent may request reassignment without proving misconduct.

Same-gender matching is an additional safeguard and does not replace visibility, parent consent, communication transparency, transportation limits, emotional boundaries, reporting duties, or regular oversight.
