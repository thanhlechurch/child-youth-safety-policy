# Section 22: Digital Communication, Social Media, Photography, and Privacy

## Section 22.1: Communication

- Use TLC-approved, archivable platforms and accounts when communicating on behalf of a ministry.

- One-to-one private communication with a minor is prohibited. Include a parent, guardian, or another approved adult in the thread or communication.

- Disappearing messages, secret accounts, requests to delete messages, and communication designed to avoid oversight are prohibited.

- Late-night communication is prohibited except for a genuine safety emergency. Ministry handbooks shall define reasonable communication hours.

- Staff and volunteers must preserve messages involving disclosures, threats, sexual content, boundary concerns, or other reportable information.

- Staff and volunteers may not send, request, possess, or forward sexualized images of minors. Suspected child sexual abuse material must be handled according to law and law-enforcement instructions; do not redistribute it internally.

## Section 22.2: Photos, video, and livestreaming

- Follow parent or guardian permissions and any no-photo restriction recorded in the attendance system.

- Use ministry-owned or approved accounts and storage whenever possible.

- Do not post identifying information, location details, medical information, or embarrassing content without authorization.

- Do not take photographs or video in restrooms, changing areas, showers, sleeping spaces, or other private settings.

## Section 22.3: Data protection

Access to rosters, phone numbers, addresses, medical information, custody alerts, background-check results, incident reports, and safeguarding records is limited to persons with a legitimate need to know. Personal data may not be copied into private contact lists or used for non-ministry purposes.
