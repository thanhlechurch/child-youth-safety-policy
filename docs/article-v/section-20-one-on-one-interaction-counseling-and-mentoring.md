# Section 20: One-on-One Interaction, Counseling, and Mentoring

Private, unobservable one-on-one contact between an adult staff member or volunteer and a minor is prohibited. Individual pastoral care may occur only under approved safeguards.

- Meet in a public or clearly observable location with an unlocked door and other adults nearby.

- Inform the parent or guardian and a supervising leader of recurring individual meetings, unless doing so would create immediate danger and civil authorities advise otherwise.

- Set reasonable limits on frequency, duration, topic, and location.

- Do not meet in bedrooms, hotel rooms, isolated homes, parked vehicles, or other secluded spaces.

- Do not promise absolute confidentiality. Explain that safety concerns may need to be shared with people who can help.

- Do not provide therapy or clinical advice beyond the staff member’s or volunteer’s training and role. Refer to qualified professionals when needed.

A staff member or volunteer who unexpectedly becomes alone with a minor must move immediately to an observable occupied area or summon another approved adult.
