# Section 5: Governance and Safeguarding Responsibilities

## Section 5.1: Minimum safeguarding leadership

The Board of Elders shall appoint at least two Designated Safeguarding Leads. At least one should be a non-staff Elder, corporate officer, Deacon, or qualified lay leader with sufficient independence to receive concerns outside the ordinary ministry chain. The two leads should not be spouses, close relatives, or persons whose shared conflict could prevent an impartial response. TLC should seek both male and female participation when practicable.

TLC may establish a larger standing safeguarding team as qualified people become available. The absence of a larger committee does not suspend reporting, protective action, documentation, recusal, or Board oversight.

## Section 5.2: Case Response Group

For a serious allegation, repeat pattern, or senior-leader concern, the Safeguarding Leads shall form a non-conflicted Case Response Group appropriate to the matter. The group may include a non-conflicted Elder or officer and outside legal, insurance, denominational, investigative, medical, mental-health, or safeguarding assistance. No person named in the concern, close associate, relative, direct subordinate, or material witness may select or direct the group.

## Section 5.3: Emergency authority

The Board shall designate at least two non-conflicted persons who may impose an immediate temporary restriction from minor-serving, leadership, preaching, teaching, transportation, counseling, discipleship, or facility-access duties when delay could create risk. One authorized person may act when immediate action is necessary, but the restriction must be reviewed by a second authorized person and the appropriate governing body as soon as practicable. A temporary restriction is protective and does not determine guilt.

## Section 5.4: Board oversight

The Board of Elders retains final authority for policy adoption, leadership qualification, employment and office decisions, and formal church discipline under TLC governing documents. Conflicted Elders must recuse themselves. The Board shall not require unanimity before a necessary civil report or immediate protective action.
