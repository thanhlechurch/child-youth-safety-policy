# Section 3: Scope and Applicability

This policy applies to every TLC-controlled or TLC-sponsored activity involving a minor or high-school participant, whether on church property, in a private home, in a vehicle, online, or at an off-site location.

## Section 3.1: Persons covered

- Senior Pastor, ministry pastors, elders, deacons, corporate officers, directors, ministry leaders, paid staff, and contractors with access to minors.

- Adult volunteers, classroom teachers, nursery volunteers, floaters, check-in and check-out volunteers, drivers, event leaders, host families, medical volunteers, security and technology personnel, photographers, and worship or production volunteers assigned to youth activities.

- Teenage helpers, junior leaders, interns, trainees, guest teachers, visiting speakers, missionaries, and occasional helpers.

- Parents or guardians who participate regularly or exercise supervisory responsibility beyond care of their own child.

- Outside ministries and organizations acting under TLC’s name, authority, sponsorship, or direct operational control.

## Section 3.2: Programs and age groups

- Nursery: newborns and infants.

- Children’s Ministry (“CM”): Pre-K through sixth grade.

- Youth Ministry: seventh through twelfth grade.

- SALT: TLC’s discipleship program in which approved adults may disciple youth in grades 7-12. Adults may not be assigned through SALT to individually disciple children in nursery or CM.

- High-school participants who have turned 18 remain subject to youth-program boundaries while participating as students. Their legal-adult status must be considered separately for screening, reporting, transportation, and room assignments.

## Section 3.3: Activities covered

- Sunday services, classes, nursery, worship, small groups, Bible studies, discipleship, counseling, mentoring, outreach, service projects, and special events.

- Vacation Bible School, camps, retreats, lock-ins, conferences, mission trips, fundraisers, home gatherings, recreation, sports, and informal ministry outings organized or represented as TLC activities.

- Transportation, carpools, hotel stays, host homes, cabins, campsites, airports, public spaces, and international travel.

- Text messages, telephone calls, email, social media, gaming platforms, group chats, video calls, ministry applications, photographs, videos, livestreams, and databases.
