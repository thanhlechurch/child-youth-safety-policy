# Section 33: Registered Sex Offenders and Other Restricted Persons

A registered sex offender or a person subject to a legal restriction involving minors may not serve, volunteer, lead, transport, counsel, disciple, photograph, or have access to minor ministry areas or information.

Attendance, if permitted at all, requires advance legal and insurance review and a written contingent-participation agreement specifying approved services, entrances, seating, supervision, prohibited areas, communications, restroom use, arrival and departure, disclosure on a need-to-know basis, and consequences for violation. The protection of children takes priority over the person’s preferred participation.
