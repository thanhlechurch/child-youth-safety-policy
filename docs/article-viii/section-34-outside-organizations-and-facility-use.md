# Section 34: Outside Organizations and Facility Use

An outside group using TLC facilities must enter a written agreement that clearly allocates responsibility for screening, supervision, reporting, insurance, and participant safety. TLC should not casually assume responsibility for another organization’s staff and volunteers or represent that it has screened or supervised them unless it has actually done so.

- TLC may require proof of insurance, child-protection policies, background screening, reporting procedures, and responsible leadership.

- Outside staff and volunteers must not access TLC minor programs, rosters, or secured areas unless separately approved.

- When TLC jointly operates or directly controls the activity, this policy applies to the extent of TLC’s role and authority.
