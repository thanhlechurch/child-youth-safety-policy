# Section 31: Confidentiality, Documentation, and Record Preservation

- Safeguarding information is shared only with persons who need it to protect minors, satisfy law, make governance decisions, provide care, or comply with insurance and legal obligations.

- Confidentiality does not mean secrecy from civil authorities or authorized decision-makers.

- Staff and volunteers shall document facts, dates, times, locations, persons present, exact or substantially exact words, actions taken, agencies contacted, report numbers, and instructions received.

- Do not include speculation, diagnosis, blame, or theological conclusions in factual incident records.

- Records must be secured separately from ordinary ministry files with controlled access and backups.

- No record may be destroyed when a report, allegation, investigation, claim, litigation hold, insurance matter, or foreseeable dispute exists.

Retention periods must be approved by counsel and the insurer. TLC should retain abuse, grooming, and serious misconduct records permanently unless counsel directs otherwise.
