# Section 18: Disability, Inclusion, and Individual Support Plans

TLC seeks to include children and youth with disabilities or additional support needs while maintaining dignity, privacy, and safety for the participant, peers, staff, and volunteers.

- Obtain parent or guardian information about communication, behavior, mobility, sensory, medical, elopement, toileting, and emergency needs.

- Create a written individual support or safety plan when ordinary procedures are insufficient.

- Assign additional trained staff or volunteers when needed rather than relying on untrained persons or a single adult.

- Do not use disability as a reason to exclude a participant without considering reasonable, safe accommodations.

- Personal care follows the two-adult, visibility, dignity, and minimum-assistance standards in this policy.
