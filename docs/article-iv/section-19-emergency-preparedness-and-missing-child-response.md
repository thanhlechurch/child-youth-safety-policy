# Section 19: Emergency Preparedness and Missing-Child Response

## Section 19.1: Emergency plans

TLC shall maintain ministry-specific plans for fire, earthquake, evacuation, shelter-in-place, lockdown, intruder or violence, severe weather, medical emergency, missing child, reunification, and communication failure. Plans must identify primary and alternate exits, assembly areas, accountability procedures, emergency equipment, and responsible leaders.

## Section 19.2: Missing child

> 1\. Immediately notify the event leader and activate the missing-child procedure; do not delay while conducting an informal search.
>
> 2\. Secure exits and assign screened adults to check designated areas without leaving other minors unsupervised.
>
> 3\. Verify the child’s last known location, clothing, medical risks, custody alerts, and authorized pickup information.
>
> 4\. Call law enforcement promptly when the child is not immediately located, abduction is possible, or circumstances indicate danger.
>
> 5\. Notify the parent or guardian and preserve attendance, video, communication, and witness information.
>
> 6\. Document the response and conduct a post-incident review.
