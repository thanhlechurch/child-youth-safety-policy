# Section 37: Policy Acknowledgment and Ministry-Specific Handbooks

Every staff member and volunteer covered by this policy shall receive and acknowledge the documents applicable to the person’s role before independent service and annually thereafter.

- Church-Wide Child and Youth Safety Policy acknowledgment.

- Church-Wide Child and Youth Ministry Code of Conduct and reporting/non-retaliation acknowledgment.

- CM Volunteer Handbook for nursery and CM staff and volunteers, including KidCheck, centralized checkout, classroom visibility, floaters, restrooms, staffing-shortage procedures, field-trip driver and passenger assignments, approved transportation communication, and final-passenger safeguards.

- Youth Ministry Volunteer Handbook for Youth staff and volunteers, including counseling, communication, routine pickup and drop-off, recurring-ride approval, approved transportation group communication, home gatherings, peer misconduct, retreats, and overnight events.

- SALT Youth Discipleship Addendum documenting TLC’s program-wide same-gender matching rule, parent consent, meeting visibility, communication transparency, transportation limits, emotional boundaries, oversight, and reassignment procedures.

- Role- or event-specific training and forms for drivers, overnight leaders, medical needs, mission trips, disability support, and other specialized duties.

- Board-approved safeguarding courses, annual certification, leader-response training, and any remediation assigned under Section 10.

Signing confirms receipt, understanding, agreement to comply, and awareness of consequences. A signature does not replace training, supervision, or monitoring and does not guarantee placement or continued service.
