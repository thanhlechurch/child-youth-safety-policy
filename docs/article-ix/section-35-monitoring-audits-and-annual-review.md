# Section 35: Monitoring, Audits, and Annual Review

- Maintain current rosters of all staff and volunteers covered by this policy, including roles, screening, assigned training, completion and renewal status, background-check status, acknowledgments, and restrictions.

- Conduct regular announced and unannounced observations of CM, Youth, SALT, check-in and release, transportation, and events.

- Review incident patterns, staffing shortages, late pickups, restroom issues, digital concerns, and policy exceptions for systemic risk.

- Report material compliance gaps to the Board of Elders and assign corrective action with deadlines.

- Review this policy at least annually with legal, insurance, and safeguarding input as appropriate.

- Document revision history, approval, communication, training, and effective dates.

## Section 35.1: Institutional learning and corrective action

When a concern reveals unclear expectations, informal handling, missing documentation, failure to assign case ownership, delayed escalation, missed acknowledgments or status updates, excessive reliance on reputation or personal trust, leadership conflicts, inappropriate contact by supporters, inadequate supervision, or other organizational weaknesses, TLC will examine the system as well as individual conduct.

The Board of Elders or its non-conflicted designees shall identify contributing failures, assign corrective actions and responsible persons, set completion dates, and verify follow-through. Corrective action may include policy revision, training, staffing changes, facility improvements, communication changes, outside review, and additional monitoring.

TLC will not treat the adoption of a new policy as a substitute for appropriately reviewing unresolved existing concerns or addressing present risk.
