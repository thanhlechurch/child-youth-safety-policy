# Appendix E: Leadership Decisions Before Adoption

- Appoint two Designated Safeguarding Leads and at least one alternate non-conflicted Elder, officer, Deacon, or qualified lay leader.

- Define who may impose an immediate temporary restriction, how a second authorized person reviews it, and the timeframe for Board review.

- Approve the procedure for forming a Case Response Group and obtaining outside assistance when TLC lacks sufficient impartial or qualified people.

- Approve the internal decision standard, written notice, response, appeal, recordkeeping, public-correction, and repeat-offense procedures in Sections 29 and 32.

- Obtain counsel’s interpretation of California Business and Professions Code section 18975, including training, background screening, the two-mandated-reporter standard, regular-volunteer thresholds, and the SALT mentoring exception.

- Select the background-check process, funding, priority schedule, renewal interval, and confidential records custodian.

- Approve the training administrator, provider-selection criteria, required course categories by role, assessment and passing standards, annual and full-retraining intervals, assignment deadlines, reminder and escalation procedures, consequences for non-completion, training budget, and secure record access and retention.

- Approve the annual leader tabletop exercise, including who facilitates it, which leaders must participate, how completion and lessons learned are documented, and how scenarios are kept separate from confidential active matters.

- Confirm current Orange County and local law-enforcement reporting contacts and the written follow-up submission process.

- Approve communication hours and platforms for Youth and SALT.

- Approve medication and emergency-medication procedures with appropriate medical, legal, and insurance input.

- Set final ratios and transitional supervision rules by age group and activity.

- Complete the second-floor emergency and safeguarding map, including restrooms, exits, stairways, AED, first aid, fire equipment, restricted areas, and assembly points.

- Clarify ministry internship, membership, and six-month participation requirements and how they interact.

- Approve record-retention periods, public communication authority, and the process for sharing relevant restrictions across ministries.

- Confirm how TLC will receive and review historical concerns, including conduct that predates policy adoption, and how current protective restrictions will be imposed and communicated.

- Establish a documented corrective-action process when institutional weaknesses contribute to a concern or allow a pattern to continue.

- Approve the TLC transportation communication channel, required trip and drop-off information, record-retention practice, and the Board-designated authority for reviewing recurring one-adult/one-youth transportation exceptions.

- Approve the case-acknowledgment, case-owner, child-and-family liaison, written safety-plan, process-update, closure-communication, delayed-case review, and independent-escalation procedures in Section 29.

- Define the approved method for documenting and enforcing direct and indirect no-contact directions, including prohibited contact through spouses, relatives, friends, supporters, intermediaries, ministry gatherings, and online channels.
