# Appendix F: Legal and Internal Development Notes

TLC developed this policy from our Constitution and Bylaws, our ministry practices, applicable California requirements, insurance and safeguarding guidance, and review of comparative church policies. Detailed research notes, source files, and drafting comparisons are maintained separately by TLC and are not part of the adopted volunteer-facing policy.

## Required legal and insurance review

- Confirm current California child-abuse reporting, youth-service-organization, training, screening, confidentiality, and record-retention requirements.

- Confirm official reporting contacts, forms, submission procedures, and emergency-response information before the effective date and at least annually.

- Review the policy with TLC’s legal counsel and liability insurer before adoption and after significant legal, insurance, ministry, or facility changes.

## TLC governing and operational materials

- Thanh Le Church Constitution and Bylaws.

- TLC nursery, CM, Youth, SALT, KidCheck, transportation, retreat, mission, facility, internship, and membership practices.

- Board decisions, leadership assignments, approved forms, training records, annual reviews, incident lessons, and revision history.
