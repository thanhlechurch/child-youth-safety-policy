# Section 7: Staff and Volunteer Classifications and Eligibility

| **Classification**                       | **Permitted role and restrictions**                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
|------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Approved adult staff member or volunteer | Completed required application, interview, references, background screening, training, acknowledgment, and ministry approval. May count toward staffing when assigned.                                                                         |
| Regular volunteer                        | Adult volunteer whose service meets the statutory or TLC-defined frequency threshold. Subject to all required screening, training, and recordkeeping.                                                                                          |
| Ministry intern / trainee                | Time-limited non-member participant. Must be screened and trained for assigned access. May not serve independently or count as a required adult unless fully approved under the same standards as an approved adult staff member or volunteer. |
| Teen helper                              | Minor serving under direct supervision. Does not replace an approved adult or count as the sole accountability person. Requires application, interview, references or leader input, training, and parent acknowledgment.                       |
| Occasional helper / classroom parent     | May assist only in observable settings under direct supervision. Does not count toward minimum staffing unless fully approved.                                                                                                                 |
| Guest teacher / speaker                  | Must remain supervised unless fully screened and approved. A speaking invitation does not authorize private access, transportation, or communication with minors.                                                                              |
| Contractor / vendor                      | Access must be limited and supervised unless the person has completed required screening for recurring access.                                                                                                                                 |
| Prospective volunteer / observer         | May observe only with approval and continuous supervision. May not assume responsibility for minors.                                                                                                                                           |

## Section 7.1: Membership and safeguarding approval

TLC membership, completion of VM or EM membership classes, family history, long attendance, or growing up in the church does not automatically authorize service with minors. Ministry eligibility and safeguarding approval are separate decisions.

TLC may permit a non-member internship of approximately three months under its approved internship program. Continued service after the internship requires compliance with TLC’s membership and ministry requirements. The internship may not be used to bypass screening, training, supervision, or conduct standards.
