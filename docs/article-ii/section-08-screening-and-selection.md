# Section 8: Screening and Selection

TLC shall screen current and prospective staff and volunteers according to their access and responsibility. No person is “grandfathered” based on years of service, family reputation, leadership title, or prior informal approval.

## Section 8.1: Minimum screening components

- Written application and signed authorization.

- Personal interview addressing motivation, ministry history, boundaries, and suitability.

- At least two reference checks for regular adult staff and volunteers, with preference for institutional references from settings where the person served minors.

- Review of prior churches, employers, ministries, residences, and service involving minors when relevant.

- Criminal-history and sex-offender screening required by law, insurer, or TLC policy.

- Review and documentation by authorized, trained personnel who protect confidential information.

## Section 8.2: Waiting period

The normal standard is that an adult volunteer has participated regularly at TLC for at least six months before receiving independent access to minors. The Board may approve a structured internship or documented exception only when screening, training, direct supervision, and risk controls are completed. Exceptions may not be granted merely because volunteers are needed.

## Section 8.3: Screening decisions

Screening is an overall suitability assessment. A clear background check does not establish suitability, and a concern that does not result in conviction may still require restriction based on credible information, role demands, or risk. Decisions must be documented, consistent, non-discriminatory, and reviewed with counsel when necessary.
